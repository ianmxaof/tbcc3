Quick Fix hotkey

Click the key box, then press a shortcut — or double-tap Shift (⇧⇧, Raycast-style). Chords need Ctrl/Shift/Alt/⌘. Esc cancels.

Dynamic tokens

Use these anywhere inside a snippet body — they're expanded at insert time.

{{date}} {{date:MM/DD/YYYY}} {{time}} {{datetime}} {{clipboard}} {{cursor}}

{{cursor}} places the caret there after expansion (plain inputs/textareas only — contenteditable fields land the caret at the end).
