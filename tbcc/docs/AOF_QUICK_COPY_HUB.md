**How money is supposed to move**

1. **Signups** — friend opens your `nodress.site` / `ref_id=` / `bot?start=` link → you get **free coins** (BotyNude: 2 coins/join).
2. **Spend share** — when referrals buy **premium coins**, you earn **~10%** into **Referral USD Balance** (BotyNude model).
3. **Withdrawal** — USD balance must hit **$10 minimum**, paid as **SOL USDT** via `/withdraw`. Until then: $0 display.

**Why USD stays $0 while rank climbs**

- Most X-driven signups use **free tiers** only → coins yes, spend share no.
- USD accrues on **paid** top-ups; volume may be there but below $10 threshold.
- Leaderboard rank reflects **referral count**, not dollars paid out.
- Payout rails (crypto, min thresholds, manual review) often lag weeks or stall — your skepticism is warranted.
